# Requesting access to an application

Your team's access bundle gives you the applications you need from day one.

For anything else, ask IT through the assistant or the IT queue. Say which application you need and why. Your manager may be asked to confirm.

Some groups give powerful access, such as production systems or admin rights. Elevated access needs IT approval, and IT may ask for a reason and an end date.
