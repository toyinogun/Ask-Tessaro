# Working from home

Most teams work from home two or three days a week. Agree the rhythm with your team; Payments and IT meet in the office on Tuesdays.

You get a one time home office budget of 500 euros for a chair, a screen or a desk. Claim it as an expense claim with the receipts.

Your home office should be quiet enough for calls and safe to work in. Lock your screen when you step away, also at home.
