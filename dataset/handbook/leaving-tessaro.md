# Leaving Tessaro

When you leave, People sets your last working day in Frappe HR. At 17:30 on that day your accounts are switched off.

Before you go:

- Hand in your laptop at the IT desk; IT raises a return ticket for it.
- Submit any open expense claims, Finance is told about claims that are still open.
- Hand over your work and any stand in duties to your manager.

Future desk bookings are cancelled automatically. People sends you your final payslip and a reference on request.
