# Laptop repair and replacement

If your laptop breaks, raise an IT ticket through the assistant or the IT queue. Describe what happens and since when.

Bring the laptop to the IT desk on the 2nd floor in Amsterdam, or to reception in Rotterdam. You get a loan laptop within 1 working day, so you can keep working while yours is repaired.

Repairs usually take one to two weeks. IT updates your ticket when your laptop is back.

Laptops are replaced every four years, or sooner when a repair is not worth it.
