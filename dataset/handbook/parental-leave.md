# Parental leave

Tessaro follows Dutch law on birth leave, additional birth leave and parental leave, and tops up paid parental leave to 70 percent of your salary for the first nine weeks.

Request parental leave in Frappe HR at least two months before it starts. People will help you plan the weeks and explain how pay works.

You can take parental leave in blocks or spread it over days of the week. Agree the pattern with your manager.
