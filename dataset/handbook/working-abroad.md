# Working abroad

You can work up to 20 working days a year from an EU country other than the Netherlands. This needs People approval in advance: send People the dates and the country at least two weeks before you go.

Working from outside the EU is possible only after a case by case review by People, because tax, insurance and data rules differ by country. Start that conversation early.

Some rules apply wherever you are:

- Work on a Tessaro laptop only, with the VPN on.
- Keep your normal working hours in Dutch time, or agree different hours with your manager.
- Do not work from a public network without the VPN.

Days abroad for business travel do not count towards the 20 days.
