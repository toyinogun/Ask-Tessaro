# Your first week

Welcome to Tessaro. Before you start, your account, laptop and desk are prepared, and your manager approves your team's access.

On your first day, come to reception at 09:30. IT hands over your laptop and helps you sign in. Your manager walks you through the team and your first tasks.

During the week:

- Set up a second factor in Authentik.
- Read the handbook pages on time off, expenses and security.
- Join your team's Zulip channels and say hello in general.
- Book your desks for the next week.

Your People advisor checks in with you at the end of the week.
