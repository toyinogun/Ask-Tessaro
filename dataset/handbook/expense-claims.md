# Expense claims

Pay for work costs yourself and claim them back. Submit a claim in ERPNext within 30 days of the cost, with receipts attached. Claims without receipts are rejected.

Your manager approves the claim. Approved claims are paid in the first payroll run at least 5 working days after approval, into the bank account Frappe HR has for you.

You can ask the assistant for the status of your own claims and the expected payment date.

What you can claim:

- Travel for work, see Business travel.
- Training and books your manager agreed to.
- Your home office budget.

What you cannot claim: fines, personal meals, and alcohol.
