# Vacation and leave types

Everyone at Tessaro gets 25 vacation days a year, allocated on 1 January. If you join during the year, you get a share for the months you work.

Request vacation in Frappe HR. Your manager approves it, or their stand in when your manager is away. Plan longer breaks at least a month ahead so the team can cover.

Unused days carry over to the next year, but try to use them: rest matters.

Other kinds of leave do not come out of your vacation days:

- Sick leave, see Reporting sick.
- Parental leave, see Parental leave.
- Special leave for moving house and family events, see Special leave.

You can ask the assistant for your own balance at any time. It shows your remaining days and any requests still waiting for approval.
