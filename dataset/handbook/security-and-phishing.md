# Security and phishing

Phishing messages try to make you click a link, share a code or act fast. Be suspicious of any message that is urgent, asks for credentials, or claims to come from IT or leadership with unusual instructions.

IT will never ask you for your password or a sign in code, and will never ask you to forward colleagues' personal data.

If something looks wrong, do not click. Report it in the IT queue. Reporting a false alarm is always fine.
