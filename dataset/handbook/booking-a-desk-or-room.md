# Booking a desk or room

Book a desk in Seatsurfing before you come to the office, so there is always a place for you. You can book up to four weeks ahead.

Meeting rooms are booked the same way. Cancel a booking you no longer need, so a colleague can use it.

New joiners get a desk booked for their first week automatically.
