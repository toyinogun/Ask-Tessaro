# Who to contact

Ask the assistant first: it can answer handbook questions and look up your own leave, claims, tickets, devices and bookings.

When you need a person, use the right queue:

- The IT queue: laptops, access, accounts and security.
- The People queue: leave, contracts, parental leave and wellbeing.
- The Finance queue: expense claims and payments.
- The Workplace queue: offices, desks, rooms and equipment.

Your manager and your People advisor are always there too.
