# Reporting sick

If you are too ill to work, tell your manager and People before 09:30 on the first day you are off. A short message in Zulip or a call is enough.

You never need to say why you are ill. Your manager and colleagues will not ask, and you do not have to tell them. If you choose to share something, People keeps it private.

There is no doctor's note for the first 7 days. If you are off for longer, People will ask for a note from your doctor and will check in with you about support.

When you are better, tell your manager you are back. People records the sick leave in Frappe HR, so you do not need to file a leave request yourself.

If you fall ill during your vacation, tell People. Days you were ill can be turned back into vacation days.
