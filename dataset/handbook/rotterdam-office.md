# Rotterdam office

Our Rotterdam office is at Kompasroosweg 37, 3011 TK Rotterdam. Finance works here most days.

The office is open from 08:00 to 18:00 on working days. There are 6 bookable desks and the Harbour room for six people.

Ask the Workplace team for a badge before your first visit.
