# Amsterdam office

Our main office is at Lantaarnvisgracht 214, 1016 XT Amsterdam.

The office is open from 08:00 to 19:00 on working days. Your badge opens the front door; reception is on the ground floor and the IT desk is on the 2nd floor.

There are 12 bookable desks and two meeting rooms, the Canal room for eight people and the Tulip room for four.

Bike parking is in the basement. The nearest tram stop is a five minute walk.
