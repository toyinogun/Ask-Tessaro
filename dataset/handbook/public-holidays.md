# Public holidays

Tessaro offices close on the Dutch national public holidays: New Year's Day, Easter Monday, King's Day, Liberation Day (every year), Ascension Day, Whit Monday, Christmas Day and Boxing Day.

If you do not celebrate one of these days, you may swap it for another day of your choice. Tell your manager and People in advance.
