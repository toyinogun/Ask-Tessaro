# Special leave

Some life events give you paid leave that does not come from your vacation days:

- Moving house: 1 day.
- Your own wedding or registered partnership: 2 days.
- The funeral of a close family member: up to 4 days.
- The birth of a grandchild: 1 day.

Request special leave in Frappe HR and pick the type that fits. For a funeral, tell your manager first and file the request when you are back.
