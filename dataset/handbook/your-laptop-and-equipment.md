# Your laptop and equipment

You get a laptop on your first day. Engineers get the engineering profile, everyone else the office profile. IT manages every laptop and keeps it up to date.

Look after it: keep it locked when you leave it, never lend it to someone outside Tessaro, and report loss or theft to IT straight away.

Need a screen, headset or keyboard? Ask the Workplace team, or claim it from your home office budget.
