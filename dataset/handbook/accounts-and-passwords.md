# Accounts and passwords

You sign in to every Tessaro application with one account, managed in Authentik.

Forgot your password? Reset it yourself through Authentik's own recovery on the sign in page. The assistant cannot reset your password, and IT will never ask you for it.

Use a password manager, and turn on a second factor in Authentik on your first day.
